# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want VIS to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Spanish and English are both first-class languages here.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful of differing opinions and experience levels.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, casiangel333, through the contact listed on the [casiangel333 GitHub profile](https://github.com/casiangel333). All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, in proportion to the harm: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
